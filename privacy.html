Privacy Policy

BrainVault Publications · Last updated: November 25, 2026

BrainVault Publications creates printable games and activity books sold on Etsy (shop: BrainVaultBooks). This site hosts images and information about our products.

Pinterest integration

We use a private tool that connects only to our own Pinterest business account through the Pinterest API to create and schedule Pins of our own products. The tool reads our own boards and creates Pins on them. It does not access, collect, store or share data about any other Pinterest user.

Information we collect

This website does not use cookies or advertising trackers. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first tool that counts visits without cookies and without identifying individual visitors. The only personal information we collect is the email address you choose to enter in our newsletter signup form. It is stored in a Google Form owned by BrainVault Publications (see Google's Privacy Policy), used only to send you BrainVault news and new products, and never sold or shared. Fonts and images are served from this site itself. Like any website, our host (Cloudflare Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Purchases are handled entirely by Etsy under Etsy's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

The only data our Pinterest tool keeps is the access token for our own Pinterest account, stored privately and deleted when the connection is revoked.

Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe. To unsubscribe or have your email deleted, reply to any of our emails or write to brainvaultbooks@gmail.com, and we will remove it.
