Privacy Policy

BrainVault Publications · Last updated: November 25, 2026

BrainVault Publications creates printable games and activity books sold on Etsy (shop: BrainVaultBooks). This site hosts images and information about our products.

Pinterest integration

We use a private tool that connects only to our own Pinterest business account through the Pinterest API to create and schedule Pins of our own products. The tool reads our own boards and creates Pins on them. It does not access, collect, store or share data about any other Pinterest user.

Information we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information. Purchases are handled entirely by Etsy under Etsy's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

The only data our tool keeps is the access token for our own Pinterest account, stored privately and deleted when the connection is revoked. You can contact us to ask any question about this policy.
